What this shows. These are net changes: forest lost to clearing, fire, or development minus forest that regrows or is planted. A country can lose old forest and still show growth here. Data is national, not by state.

Where the data comes from. Live requests to the open World Bank Indicators API, using the indicators AG.LND.FRST.K2 (forest area, sq km) and AG.LND.FRST.ZS (forest area, % of land). The World Bank sources these from the UN Food and Agriculture Organization.
